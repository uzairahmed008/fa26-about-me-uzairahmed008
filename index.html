The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Accompanied by Dr. Watson, Holmes investigates unusual crimes, uncovers hidden clues, and reveals surprising truths. The stories continue to influence mystery and detective fiction today.

The adventures are known for clever plots, memorable characters, and detailed investigations that challenge readers to solve the mystery alongside Holmes.
